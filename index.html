Uncertainty that survives being checked

I build generative and field-level inference pipelines for cosmology — diffusion models that sample posteriors over the CMB, stacking frameworks that report their nulls honestly — and then spend most of the effort trying to break them. Probabilistic field-level CMB component separation

A conditional DDPM in the Haar wavelet domain, conditioned on nine Planck PR3 channels, sampling p(CMB | observations) with conformal coverage certification. An open-loop evaluation against injected PySM3 truth reverses the apparent model ranking, and a four-way ablation localises the calibration deficit to the diffusion forward process itself.

SPHEREx × HETDEX cross-matching and spectral stacking

Rest-frame optical lines at z ≈ 2.5, stacked at 565 band-complete SPHEREx positions. A clean null after zodiacal subtraction with 3σ upper limits, a band-count selection effect that biases any SPHEREx multi-band stack, and an external check on the SPHEREx ZODI model across 67° of ecliptic latitude.
